# the mindful leader - reminders

_previous note:_ [[Carroll - The Mindful Leader]]

1. **Open before acting.**  
   See what is actually happening, not what you wish were happening.

2. **Drop the cape.**  
   Notice when you're defending your status, opinions or sense of self.

3. **Attend to the work, not success.**  
   Slow down. Appreciate what the situation needs.

4. **Let go of emotional victory.**  
   You don't need to win the argument or have the last word.

5. **Listen to understand.**  
   Be more interested in what someone means than in making your point.

6. **Trust your basic intelligence.**  
   You need neither to announce yourself nor apologise for yourself.

7. **Make room for uncertainty.**  
   Release fixed views. Stay curious, flexible and willing to be surprised.

8. **Take responsibility. Give praise away.**  
   Own mistakes without defensiveness. Recognise the contributions of others.

9. **Lead for health, not just results.**  
   Cultivate trust, respect, care and the conditions in which others flourish.

10. **Be fully human.**  
    Bring warmth, humour, courage and gentleness to whatever arises.
