# Zen meditation as embodied transformation

## Quotation

> Zen Buddhism, this seemingly old-fashioned tradition from the East, has become so important to me because it stubbornly insists on embodiment. In Zen, conceptual understanding is never enough; transformation needs to manifest in, through, and as the body. By its own account, as a school of Buddhism, Zen has emphasized the bodily practice of meditation over the mental effort of conceptual study.

Dillo, Christian (2022). *The Path of Aliveness: A Contemporary Zen Approach to Awakening Body and Mind*. Shambhala. ISBN 9781611809978. [Publisher's page](https://www.shambhala.com/the-path-of-aliveness-9781611809978.html).

## Context and relevance

**Source-based observation:** In the supplied passage, Dillo contrasts conceptual study with bodily meditation practice and maintains that transformation needs to manifest bodily. The passage expresses his account of Zen, not a claim established here about all traditions of Zen Buddhism.
