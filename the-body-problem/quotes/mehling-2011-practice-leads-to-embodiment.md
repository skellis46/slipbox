# Mehling – practice leads to embodiment and integration

## Quotation

> So through repetition and training in these skills of noticing, differentiating, and discerning, individuals engaged in mind-body practices that involve the body are immersed in a process that leads to embodiment and integration.

Mehling, W. E., Wrubel, J., Daubenmier, J. J., Price, C. J., Kerr, C. E., Silow, T., Gopisetty, V. and Stewart, A. L. (2011). ## Context

**Source-based paraphrase:** In the same Results discussion, practitioners describe noticing sensations, thoughts and feelings in their immediacy, and learning differentiated noticing, as a path to integration (literature note, p. 6). The passage summarises that view: training in noticing leads to embodiment and integration.

## Relevance

**Recorded 19 November 2026. No boundary test opened.**

**LLM interpretations (provisional, not agreed):**
- **"Leads to":** embodiment here is an achievement or outcome of practice, not a condition. This appears to oppose BT-04's agreed distinction between transformations *within* and increases *in* embodiment. Apply BT-03's lesson first: Mehling's use of 'embodiment' is therapeutic, developmental and normative; ours is descriptive. The opposition may be terminological.
- **BT-04:** strongest link, but BT-04 is provisionally closed and should not be reopened merely because a source restates the larger question.
- **BT-05:** Merleau-Ponty's habit makes the body unnoticed; Mehling's training makes it noticed. Mehling et al.'s four-stage model places 'the lived body', taken for granted and 'absent', at the first and least integrated stage, while our map's lived body / phenomenology conception treats the lived body as embodiment itself. A possible tension within one conception.
- **BT-06:** 'skills of noticing, differentiating, and discerning' resembles Ingold's education of attention.
- **C08:** relevant to cultivated noticing, but Mehling's context is therapy with health outcomes, unlike the autotelic practice C08 concerns.
- **C09:** best fit. Supplies the new case C09 required.
