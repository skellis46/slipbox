# The body as a limit of moral concern

## Quotation

> Our attitude toward nature is important. First, it is a source of moral formation. For example, maximizing the extraction of natural resources for immediate gain degrades the individual’s sense of care for the flourishing of others and of future generations by making his chief aim and goal extend no further than his own body

Fechtel, John (2025). ‘Why does it matter what you think about nature?’ *Nature/Culture* (Substack), 30 September 2025. https://fechtel.substack.com/p/why-does-it-matter-what-you-think

## Context

**Source-based paraphrase:** Fechtel argues that attitudes towards nature shape moral and intellectual habits. This passage presents resource extraction for immediate gain as an example of self-limiting concern that neglects other people and future generations. His reference to ‘his own body’ indicates a narrow boundary of self-interest; it is not presented as a formal definition of embodiment.
