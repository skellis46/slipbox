# Possible future boundary test: Somatic expertise

Status: **question reserved for separate consideration, not yet numbered or agreed**  
Identified: 8 October 2026

## The issue

How might the embodiment map accommodate forms of expertise cultivated in somatic practices when the practice is not organised around an externally specified movement outcome?

This arose during discussion of Shaun Gallagher's account of *skilled performance*, which concerns, in much of the relevant research, identifiable outcome-oriented practices or events. Do not make that literature the standard by which the expertise of all somatic practices is judged, or assert that skilled performance always involves measurable outcomes.

## Bounded example and source

Simon Ellis's working chapter [*Threshold of the Self: Dancing and Non-dual Awareness*](../../improvisation%20and%20the%20threshold%20of%20the%20self.md) describes authentic movement practised with Katye Coe as **autotelic**: without a predetermined sequence or conventional external standard of successful movement. It asks what kind of skill or expertise consists in attending to sensation, allowing movement to emerge and withholding judgement. The chapter also distinguishes *deep sensory dancing* from *being danced* and acknowledges that its interpretations of experience remain underdetermined.

The question concerns **how expertise is understood and recognised**, not a declaration that somatic expertise has no observable aspects or cannot be researched, evaluated or measured.

## Next step (not yet authorised as an active test)

When ready, agree a question and a bounded comparison. Consider the map's conceptions and knowledge modes without conflating expertise with awareness or consciousness. Identify failure points, including cases where outward observation does not capture the experience or the practice's own terms of value.

This is **separate from BT-03**, which is about whether awareness is necessary to particular conceptions of embodiment.
