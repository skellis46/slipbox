# The body problem

Updated: 26 November 2026

This folder is the working record of *The Body Problem*, a chapter by SE (Simon Ellis) and SdH (Scott deLahunta) for *Embodiment and Dance: A Reader on Somatic Thinking, Practice and Research*. It is a record of a process, not a finished chapter. *This README was written by LLM (Claude) at SE's request, 26 November 2026.*

## Start here

- **Where things stand now:** [current summary](current-summary.md).
- **What has changed since last time:** [updates for SdH](updates%20for%20SdH.md) and [updates for SE](updates%20for%20SE.md).
- **Why the process changed when it did:** [lab book](lab-book.md).
- **What to do next:** [to do](todo.md).

The project works in two parallel ways. SE tests a provisional map of embodiment against examples (the boundary tests), working with an LLM. SdH works with dance's trade literature, mostly printed books that are not digitised (`analogue_trade/`).

## Conventions

UK English, no Oxford commas, no em-dashes. Record what was **agreed**, **suggested** and **left open** separately. Cite identifiable sources accurately. Do not silently rewrite an earlier formulation, conflate an author's work with the practices it describes or treat absent historical evidence as evidence of absent experience.

Working convention (moved from the 16 October 2026 historical section): always name and briefly characterise the relevant map conception, name the specific author's account and briefly justify its selection. Mark source quotations, source-based paraphrases, **SE** proposals, **LLM** interpretations and findings agreed by SE and the LLM distinctly. The conception labels are provisional map categories devised by SE and LLM (ChatGPT), not Lux et al.'s categories. Stop at a defensible provisional finding rather than pursuing every emerging question; unresolved is a legitimate result.

### Pronouns and attribution (agreed by SE, 26 November 2026)

- "We", "our" and "us" mean SE and SdH only. Any other combination is named, for example "SE and LLM (ChatGPT)".
- Co-authors are referred to by initials: **SE** and **SdH**.
- Text written by an LLM is labelled, naming the model where known: "LLM (ChatGPT)", "LLM (Claude)".
- Instructions addressed to an LLM are kept in [llm-handoff.md](llm-handoff.md), not in this README.
- Signed first-person notes (the meeting notes and the Nutshell note) are left as written.
