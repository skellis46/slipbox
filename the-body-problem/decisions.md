# Decisions, provisional positions and open questions

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This is a log of intellectual decisions, not a record of everything discussed. Where agreement is unclear, the status is **provisional**, not “decided”.

## Agreed

- Explore both coverage of the existing embodiment maps **and** their underlying conceptual limits.
- Work slowly and carefully, without losing the original premises or introducing classifications prematurely.
- Counterfactual cases must genuinely be able to remain outside the map. An endlessly accommodating scheme is of limited value.
- Neither 'the body' in general nor explicit use of the term 'embodiment' supplies an adequate boundary on its own.
- The present task is to test boundaries, **not** populate the maps with more literature.
- Use repeatable, **one-at-a-time** boundary tests.
- Boundary test 01 is titled **Relational bodily encounters** and uses the exact agreed question quoted in its file.
- The project record should live in Markdown under the Obsidian vault's existing GitHub repository, with explicitly recorded updates.

## Provisional working positions

- The 'bodily consequentiality' formulation in the README is a prompt for investigation, not a definition of embodiment.
- Being, becoming, experiencing and acting should not yet be divided into inclusion and exclusion categories.
- Touch may involve distinct sensing, sense-making and meaning for different participants. These terms should not be treated as interchangeable.
- A map's failure to display a distinction is not automatically a conceptual incompatibility.
- Existing embodiment scholarship addresses relationality and intersubjectivity. Do not claim those are simply absent.
- Chinese medicine is not a single counterfactual case. Specific histories, texts or practices need separate examination.
- Hsu's historical account of tactile diagnosis and contemporary Functional Integration are possible **test cases**; they are not interchangeable evidence.

## Open questions

1. What grounds admission to the map as distinct from selection for testing at its margins?
2. Does the provisional formulation make embodiment merely instrumental to other phenomena?
3. Can the map represent differently situated bodily knowing without making unwarranted assumptions about reciprocity?
4. What constitutes a *conceptual failure* rather than lack of visual resolution?
5. Should ethics and other transversal concerns be represented at all, and if so how?
6. When does translation into embodiment vocabulary misrepresent a tradition's own categories?

## Not decided

- No final result for BT-01.
- No addition to the existing map's categories, axes or literature.
- No settled definition of embodiment.
- No claim that early Chinese pulse diagnosis falls outside an embodiment framework.
