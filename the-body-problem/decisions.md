# Decisions, provisional positions and open questions

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This is a log of intellectual decisions, not a record of everything discussed. Where agreement is unclear, the status is **provisional**, not “decided”.

## Agreed

- Explore both coverage of the existing embodiment maps **and** their underlying conceptual limits.
- Work slowly and carefully, without losing the original premises or introducing classifications prematurely.
- Counterfactual cases must genuinely be able to remain outside the map. An endlessly accommodating scheme is of limited value.
- Neither 'the body' in general nor explicit use of the term 'embodiment' supplies an adequate boundary on its own.
- The present task is to test boundaries, **not** populate the maps with more literature.
- Use repeatable, **one-at-a-time** boundary tests.
- Boundary test 01 is titled **Relational bodily encounters** and uses the exact agreed question quoted in its file.
- The project record should live in Markdown under the Obsidian vault's existing GitHub repository, with explicitly recorded updates.
- Continue with BT-02 using McGeer's passive dynamic walker, asking which conception of embodiment makes that description possible and what such a conception might exclude.
- BT-02 provisional outcome agreed: the passive walker fits bodily constraint / affordance, but its location within Lux's biological levels is uncertain. This may reveal a limitation in how the map's dimensions are combined, rather than exclusion from embodiment.
- No account is required to fit every dimension. Record the map's 'failure points' or poor fits. These may disclose limits of the map or illuminate the conceptions and assumptions being mapped; a non-applicable dimension is not the same as a strained or distorting classification.

## Provisional working positions

- The 'bodily consequentiality' formulation in the README is a prompt for investigation, not a definition of embodiment.
- Being, becoming, experiencing and acting should not yet be divided into inclusion and exclusion categories.
- Touch may involve distinct sensing, sense-making and meaning for different participants. These terms should not be treated as interchangeable.
- A map's failure to display a distinction is not automatically a conceptual incompatibility.
- Existing embodiment scholarship addresses relationality and intersubjectivity. Do not claim those are simply absent.
- Chinese medicine is not a single counterfactual case. Specific histories, texts or practices need separate examination.
- Hsu's historical account of tactile diagnosis and contemporary Functional Integration are possible **test cases**; they are not interchangeable evidence.
- Within some conceptions, embodiment need not require life or subjective experience. Do not convert this into a universal proposition about all embodiment scholarship.
- The passive walker troubles normative understandings of embodiment, but a map that admits everything would have little discriminatory force. This tension remains productive and unresolved.
- In BT-02, distinguish bodily organisation participating in generating behaviour from whether a system has awareness. These are different questions; the former does not settle the latter.
- A somatic standpoint that foregrounds bodily sensitivity and felt sense is a productive but situated perspective, not a criterion to impose on all conceptions of embodiment.

## Open questions

1. What grounds admission to the map as distinct from selection for testing at its margins?
2. Does the provisional formulation make embodiment merely instrumental to other phenomena?
3. Can the map represent differently situated bodily knowing without making unwarranted assumptions about reciprocity?
4. What constitutes a *conceptual failure* rather than lack of visual resolution?
5. Should ethics and other transversal concerns be represented at all, and if so how?
6. When does translation into embodiment vocabulary misrepresent a tradition's own categories?
7. How should physical coupling, environmental sensitivity and awareness be distinguished? Might this warrant another boundary test, rather than extending BT-02?
8. How should we annotate and interpret failure points consistently without treating every poor fit as a reason to change the map?
9. BT-03 (proposed): How can the map distinguish environmental responsiveness, bodily awareness without reflective attention and deliberate somatic attention, without making awareness universally necessary?

### 2026-10-08 15:32 BST | Provisional | BT-03

**Position under examination, not agreed as a definition:** One co-author leans towards treating *awareness* as synonymous with *consciousness*, distinct from *perception* (which can operate without consciousness). Body and environment may then be different contents rather than kinds of awareness. This calls the original proposed BT-03 question into doubt.

**Source-based methodological observation:** After reading Ellis's working chapter *Threshold of the Self*, the useful comparison may concern expertise in a specific autotelic practice of authentic movement, where sustained sensing, attention and allowing movement to emerge have no pre-specified movement outcome. Do not generalise this to all somatic practice or assume somatic expertise cannot be evaluated. Gallagher (2011) should not be portrayed as requiring measurable outcomes for all skill.

**Proposed alternative question, not yet accepted:** Can the map accommodate conceptions of embodiment in which awareness, provisionally understood as consciousness, is central to the practice, while the expertise being cultivated concerns attending, sensing and allowing rather than achieving an externally specified movement outcome?

### 2026-10-14 09:44 BST | Agreed and provisional | Project-wide / BT-03

**Agreed:** The initial use of Lux et al. (2021) as the basis for the maps was exploratory and somewhat arbitrary, motivated by curiosity. The maps are working instruments, not taxonomies bound by fidelity to Lux et al. Methodological rigour should be proportionate and support progress.

**Agreed:** Case choice materially affects the enquiry. Whether an account falls within, at the edge of or outside the map must be established through examination, not preassigned as a selection result. Record why cases are chosen, without assuming the comparison's outcome.

**Provisional scope, not settled:** For now, examine accounts within and at the edges of the map, allowing genuinely outside accounts to emerge; a more explicit search outside may come later. The extent to which this should be the guiding scope remains open. Retain the uncertainty rather than silently treating this as an exclusion rule.

**Practical direction:** Use a proportionate, transparent selection of a few identifiable primary accounts rather than elaborate sampling machinery. The four-case scheme already noted in BT-03 is a proposal, not a requirement or an agreed set.

### 2026-10-14 | Agreed | BT-03 suspension and methodological lesson

**Decision:** Suspend BT-03 without a substantive finding about awareness, consciousness or the map. The preliminary comparison moved between awareness, consciousness, conscious experience, deliberate attention and reportability without sufficient conceptual differentiation. The four candidate texts and their preliminary readings remain as exploratory material, not evidence of a completed test.

**Clarification:** The proposed equivalence of *consciousness* and *awareness* is an existing position or usage worth investigating, **not an original claim belonging to the co-authors**. It is distinct from equating awareness with *conscious experience*. Whether this equivalence is useful in relation to embodiment or to the map may be examined later, without presupposing its validity. No new boundary test is authorised here.

**Agreed methodological lesson for subsequent tests:** Before treating a concept as a cross-account discriminator, establish whether its meanings and uses are sufficiently comparable in the actual sources. Otherwise apparent contrasts may derive from the researchers' shifting terminology rather than differences between accounts. The map has not thereby been shown to fail; awareness may be beyond this map's current scope, but that remains provisional.

### 2026-10-14 10:27 BST | Agreed and proposed | BT-04

**Agreed:** Distinguish *degrees of embodiment* (whether something can meaningfully be more or less embodied) from *degrees of bodily involvement* (whether bodily participation or contribution varies without embodiment itself admitting of degrees). This distinction is worth testing, not an established result.

**Provenance:** Rebecca Solnit's observation in *A Field Guide to Getting Lost* that writing is 'the most disembodied art' was **only the provocation** from which the question emerged. BT-04 is not an evaluation of her comparison between arts and must not treat the quotation as evidence for a scale of embodiment.

**Proposed question, not yet agreed:** 'Can something be more or less embodied?' No cases, method or finding agreed. See [BT-04](boundary-tests/BT-04-degrees-of-embodiment.md).
