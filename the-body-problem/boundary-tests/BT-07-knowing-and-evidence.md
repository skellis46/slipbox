**Summary** *(LLM (Claude), 19 and 26 November 2026)*: researchers who use their own practice, such as dancing, as a way of doing research often distinguish different kinds of knowing: knowing *how* (skill), knowing *what* works (through reflecting on the practice) and knowing *that* (facts and ideas). This test uses Robin Nelson's account of these and asks whether the map can show how kinds of knowing relate to the evidence for them.

**Origin** *(LLM (Claude) from the records, confirmed by SE, 26 November 2026)*: BT-05 left a deferred question: the map's evidence and knowledge modes name how something is investigated, not what kind of knowing is involved. This became candidate C07. On 19 November C07 was split in two. C07a, whether one map dimension can represent kinds of knowing and their evidence, became BT-07. C07b, whether the map as a whole assumes propositional knowledge is the norm, is parked.

**Where it stands** *(LLM (Claude), 19 and 26 November 2026)*: the map's key lists "types of evidence or knowledge", which can suggest the two are the same thing. So it cannot show Nelson's main point, that the practice itself is both evidence and a way of knowing. A proposed rewording of the key removes that problem but reveals that the map has nowhere to show kinds of knowing at all. The test stays open until SdH gives a view on the proposed change.

## Agreed question

> Can the map's evidence and knowledge modes represent Nelson's relation between modes of knowing and their evidence, including his treatment of praxis as both knowing and primary evidence?

## Outcome criteria (agreed by SE and LLM, fixed before testing)

- **Fit:** the map can show know-how, know-what and know-that, how they converge as evidence and where praxis serves as both knowing and evidence.
- **Representational limitation:** the map can locate these but cannot show their relation or where they merge.
- **Conceptual boundary:** the map merges or separates knowing and evidence where Nelson does not; for example, reducing know-how to 'practice-based / pedagogical knowledge' as one method among six.

These criteria were fixed to limit post hoc interpretation, a problem recognised across earlier tests.

## Primary source

Nelson, R. (2022). *Practice as Research in the Arts (and Beyond): Principles, Processes, Contexts, Achievements*. 2nd edn. Palgrave Macmillan. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-90542-2

Chosen as the single primary source because Nelson himself relates kinds of knowing to evidence, reducing the degree to which the distinction is the interpretation of SE and LLM. The 2022 edition was preferred to the 2013 edition, which SE judged dated in relation to practice research; the 2022 edition retains the know-how / know-what / know-that model.

**Source-based claims (to verify against a legitimate copy, with page numbers):**
- Praxis is submitted as primary and substantial evidence of the research inquiry.
- Know-that is not privileged but set in dialogue with know-how (experience of practices) and know-what (critical reflection).
- The modes form an 'intra-active mix'.

Earlier statement of the model: Nelson, R. (2013). *Practice as Research in the Arts: Principles, Protocols, Pedagogies, Resistances*. Palgrave Macmillan, section 2.2.

## Named challenge (not a primary source)

Borgdorff, H. (2012). *The Conflict of the Faculties: Perspectives on Artistic Research and Academia*. Leiden University Press. Borgdorff locates artistic research in non-conceptual content and 'thinking in, through, and with art' rather than formal knowledge. **LLM interpretation:** this questions whether sorting by kinds of knowing already presumes the frame C07b examines. If BT-07 finds a fit, ask whether it depends on Nelson's scheme already presenting knowing in a form the map recognises.

## Boundaries

- BT-07 tests one map dimension. It does **not** test whether the map as a whole presumes propositional knowledge (C07b).
- A BT-07 result must not be read as answering C07b.
- Pakes (2003, 2009) is a candidate source for C07b, not BT-07.
- General epistemology (Ryle; Stanley and Williamson 2001) is used only for framing; the test does not go into it.

## Provenance of the dimension under test (19 November 2026)

- **SE account:** the six evidence / knowledge modes were devised with LLM (ChatGPT) while drafting the maps. The reference set began with Lux et al.'s citations and was later expanded. Only the seven levels come from Lux et al. (2021).
- **Secondary summary by LLM (ChatGPT)** (supplied by SE; a reconstruction of earlier chats, not a transcript): the dimension was meant to ask "How is embodiment known or made credible?"; early conceptions included "practice" and "method/epistemology", which were later dropped. Treat as uncertain.
- **SE correction:** the marker shape was deliberately added as "a type of evidence or knowledge". The summary's narrower phrase ("ways of producing or substantiating knowledge") does not match that intention.
- **Withdrawn LLM claims:** (1) that the dimension's "or" was careless; (2) that practice-based knowledge appearing only with dance and somatics sources reveals the body problem in the map. This pattern is better explained by how the reference set was assembled. (3) An LLM reading that applied the dimension's "or" to the pentagon label; SE rejected this, noting that "pedagogical knowledge" has nothing to do with praxis as evidence.

## Method: two-stage placement (agreed by SE and LLM)

To avoid revising the map and then confirming the revision, Nelson's model was placed twice: against the map as drawn (baseline) and against a proposed revised key.

## Stage 1: current map (baseline finding agreed by SE and LLM)

*Key to the shapes on the map (added by LLM (Claude), 26 November 2026): pentagon = "practice-based or pedagogical knowledge"; star = "theoretical or conceptual work"; triangle = "self-report or first-person data".*

| Nelson's element | Current map | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know-how | Pentagon | Fits, as practice-based knowledge |
| Know-that | Star (theoretical / conceptual) | Fits |
| Know-what | Triangle or star | Strained |
| Praxis as evidence | Pentagon, via the dimension's "or" | Accommodated; not an omission (SE: a seventh shape is unnecessary) |
| Praxis as evidence *and* knowing | (none) | Not held: conceptual boundary |
| Convergence of modes | (none) | Cannot be shown (one shape per reference) |

> **Finding:** On the current map, Nelson's model does not fit. The key labels one dimension "type of evidence or knowledge", which can suggest that evidence and knowledge are the same thing. Nelson treats them as different but connected. So the map cannot show his central claim, that the practice is both the evidence and a way of knowing. His other modes of knowing mostly fit, but critical reflection (know-what) has no clear place, and the map cannot show how the modes work together. The test also exposed a second wording problem. "Practice-based or pedagogical knowledge" uses a contested term and mixes research with teaching. These problems are in how SE and LLM (ChatGPT) built the map.

The conceptual-boundary reading is SE's ("as if they are somehow the same thing... when they might be and they might not be").

## Proposed map change (SE proposal, not agreed with SdH)

- Dimension title: "type of evidence or knowledge" → **"How claims are investigated and supported"**. "Method" was considered but would have predetermined the stage-two result.
- Pentagon: "practice-based or pedagogical knowledge" → **"practice-based"**.
- Parked: consistency of the other five labels ("measurement", "data", "work").

## Stage 2: revised key (finding agreed by SE and LLM)

| Nelson's element | Revised key | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Praxis as evidence | Practice-based | Fits clearly |
| Praxis as evidence *and* knowing | Practice-based | No longer blocked; the key is silent on knowing |
| Know-how | (none) | Moves off this dimension |
| Know-that | Star | Fits |
| Know-what | Triangle or star | Still strained |
| Convergence | (none) | Still cannot be shown |

> **Finding:** With the revised key, the map no longer suggests that evidence and knowledge are the same. Practice now clearly counts as a way of supporting claims. But Nelson's kinds of knowing no longer have a place on this dimension, and nowhere else on the map takes them up. Reflection (know-what) is still awkward, and the map still cannot show how different modes work together. The revision makes the map more honest about what this dimension does. It also shows that the map has no dimension for kinds of knowing.

**Boundary with C07b:** the map *lacks* a place for kinds of knowing. This is not a finding that it presumes propositional knowledge as the norm.

**Possible chapter link (LLM interpretation, not agreed):** equating evidence with knowledge may be a small instance of the assumption raised in BT-06, that knowing counts once it can be detached and shown.
