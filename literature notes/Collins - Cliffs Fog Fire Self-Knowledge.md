Barbara McClintock’s life beautifully illustrates one of the most essential findings that showed up across the entire set of lives in the study. She answered the question of what to make of a life partly by bringing together a triad of mutually reinforcing elements: She discovered and deployed a set of her encodings. She flipped the arrow of money. She focused the inner fire.


Encodings are durable capacities of a person’s intrinsic construction that lie within, awaiting discovery through the experiences of life.


The point is not that McClintock was a genius (though she was that) but that she instinctively played to deeply encoded ways of operating that suited her exceptionally well.


The question is not whether a person has encodings. Everyone has encodings. The question is this: Which encodings will the journey of life lead you to discover, and will you trust them enough to align your life around them?
