# Schneider-Bateman - What's in My NOW

_previous note:_ [[Burkeman - Four thousand weeks]]

> N-1
>
> Call it Buddhist, Stoic, existentialist, or whatever, this is my shorthand for capturing the idea that we will do everything in our lives a finite number of times and that eventually we will do everything a final time. I tattooed it on my arm. It helps me more deeply appreciate the good moments, recognize that even the challenging things will end, and reminds me that there will eventually be a final time. And, sadly, we often don’t know when that is. Is it now? Sam Harris’s [Last Time meditation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jue3XOB_y80) from his excellent Waking Up app conveys this better than I can.
