# White - The Stories We Tell Ourselves

_previous note:_ 

> **The Leadership Reckoning**
>
> This is the call for us as leaders. Not to be perfect, but to be curious. To confront our own mental fiction factories with honesty. To ask:
>
> •   What assumptions am I making without evidence?
> •   What stories have I invented to feel safe or certain?
> •   What decisions have I made based on convenience, not clarity?
>
> When we stop questioning, we start reacting. And reactive leaders rarely lead—they follow fear, familiarity, or inertia. But if we’re willing to pause, to suspend the rush to narrative, something remarkable happens. We create space for truth to enter. For connection to return. For decisions to reflect reality, not reaction.
