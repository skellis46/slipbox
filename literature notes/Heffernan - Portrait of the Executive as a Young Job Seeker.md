# Heffernan - Portrait of the Executive as a Young Job Seeker

_previous note:_ 

> **How should kids and parents think now about their education?** At risk of sounding glib, I’d say the most important thing to leave school with is a great love of learning and the capacity to pursue it. Nobody knows what skills you will need 20, 30 years from now. Everyone and everything will change, adapt and change again. If you’re good at that, and can be positive about it, you have good reason for confidence.

> My father, who wasn’t prone to good advice, once made an exception. It doesn’t much matter what you do, he said, as long as you like the people you work with.
