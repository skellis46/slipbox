# Janjigian - Sift-and-Scald for Whole-Wheat Doughs

_previous note:_ [[Janjigian - Ash Wednesday]]

> There are a couple of 100% whole-wheat formulas in the book, but both of them use a "sift-and-scald" approach (see photo above for an example), where I sift off the bran, combine it with boiling water, and let it sit awhile. I then mix the bran-less dough to full development, and add the scald at the end.

_reference:_ Andrew Janjigian, Wordloaf. "Friday Bread Basket 7/25/25." https://readwise.io/bookreview/53649334
