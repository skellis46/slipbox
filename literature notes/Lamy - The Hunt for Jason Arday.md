# Lamy - The Hunt for Jason Arday

_previous note:_ 

> Two things can be true at once: a harasser can also be perfectly gentlemanly. As race and performance scholars Kyla Wazana Tompkins and Tavia Nyong’o have argued, civility operates as a “covert political aesthetic” and a “rhetorical mode which guards the boundaries of access to politicity”: it helps determine whose speech and conduct are recognised, in the first place, as legitimate political participation. Harris’s “softly phrased” manner helped make him legible as reasonable: measured, rational and therefore credible, irrespective of the cumulative effect of his conduct.
