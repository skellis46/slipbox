# White - The Curiosity Gap

_previous note:_ [[White - When We Install Intelligence Into a System We Don't Understand]]

> Success stories aren’t coming from the companies with the biggest budgets. They’re coming from those with the most rigorous questions. The ones that spent time understanding what *actually* needed solving before jumping to implement AI.

> The organizations succeeding today aren’t just using AI. They’re redesigning how they think. They’ve cultivated **systemic curiosity**—a commitment to learning, listening, experimenting, and evolving. Not once. Continuously.
>
> Their advantage isn’t technical. It’s cultural.
