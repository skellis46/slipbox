# Kucirkova - It’s not just screen time - the problem with children’s media is that it has become relentlessly hectic, loud and bright

_previous note:_ [[L. M. Sacasas - Children and Technology]]

> Different media have different temporal demands: a digital picture book, a game, a television episode and an app each ask a child to attend differently. But, across media, the important question is whether the pace suits the experience that the narrative claims to support. Does it give children time to follow a story, grasp a concept, identify with a character, regulate emotion or share attention with another person? Or does it continually hurry them toward the next cue, the next reward, the next burst of stimulation?

> To develop empathy in the early years, a child must notice that another person feels something different. That small act of crossing over takes time.

> Is the child a consumer to be retained, a nervous system to be stimulated, a vessel to be filled with educational content – or a person whose attention is still forming, and who therefore needs time to arrive?
