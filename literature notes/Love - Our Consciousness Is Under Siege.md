# Love - Our Consciousness Is Under Siege

_previous note:_ [[Pollan - A World Appears]]

> There’s a line at the end of the book from a poem by Jorie Graham, which had a huge effect on me: “This is what is wrong: we, only we, the humans, can retreat from ourselves and not be altogether here.”

> When you form these relationships with them, they’re not real relationships. They’re sycophantic, there’s no friction. In every human relationship, even a loving relationship, there’s friction. That friction is what helps us define our identities and realize what we think. You don’t get that with a chatbot; they suck up to you.

_reference:_ Shayla Love, The Guardian. "‘Our Consciousness Is Under Siege’: Michael Pollan on Chatbots, Social Media and Mental Freedom." https://www.theguardian.com/wellness/2026/mar/05/michael-pollan-book-a-world-appears-consciousness-hygiene
