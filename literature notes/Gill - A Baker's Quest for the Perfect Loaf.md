# Gill - A Baker's Quest for the Perfect Loaf

_previous note:_ [[Franks - Existential Bread]]

> No matter how ugly or clumsy that loaf may seem now, the entire experience of making it was an epiphany: here was something, finally something, that was honest. Here was something that I made all by myself, with my very own hands, which I could trust. A whole new way of feeling the world had been born.

> This is why, each time I bake, I want to make the perfect loaf. Not because it’s possible, but because that’s love: to give so much that you forget yourself in the giving. I became a great baker, not just a good one, when I understood that the human need to share and to create–no matter our scars, no matter the outcome–is perfect enough.
