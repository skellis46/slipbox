# Masnick - AI Might Be Our Best Shot at Taking Back the Open Web

_previous note:_ [[Dash - What Would Good AI Look Like]]

> On the training data question — which is a legitimate concern whether or not you think training on copyrighted works is fair use — efforts like Common Corpus are building large-scale training sets from public domain and openly licensed materials. Anil Dash has been writing about what “[good AI](https://www.anildash.com/2025/05/01/what-would-good-ai-look-like/)” looks like in practice — AI that’s transparent about its training data, that respects consent, that minimizes externalities rather than ignoring them. There are ways to do this right.
