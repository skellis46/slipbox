# Wolf and Cohen - Humans Aren't Machines

_previous note:_ [[Challenger - Deskilling the Body]]

> Scientists in Finland were the first to prove, only about 12 years ago, the profound health benefits of the sauna and the cold plunge. Before that, it was actually thought that the sauna was really dangerous for your heart. In fact, it’s great for heart health, your lungs, and preventing dementia. Scientists in Japan, around 15 years ago, were the first to prove the physiological benefits of forest bathing, or time in nature. If you spend just 20 minutes in the green space of a park, not even wilderness, you will lower your cortisol, your inflammation, your blood pressure. You’ll improve your mood and your sleep. If you increase it to an hour, you’ll increase your working memory by 20 percent.

> **GARY:** In your everyday life you’re not submitting your ideas to peer review, nor are you waiting 5 or 10 or 15 years for them to become consensus knowledge. So I use science as a synonym for empirical practice: observing, reasoning, and explaining.

> **JUNE:** For centuries, we’ve modeled ourselves after machines. We’re supposed to be productive, efficient, and logical. But humans aren’t machines. Our human operating system is fleshy, emotional, and illogical, and we’ve kind of forgotten how to operate it. But the beauty is it’s easy to remember, and it’s joyful. When we sing, dance, laugh, feast together, we literally bond with each other. I really hope we remember that we don’t have to agree on everything. We can go out, sing, dance, and laugh together, and trust each other more the next day.
