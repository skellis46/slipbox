# Popova - How to Be an Instrument of Kindness in a Harsh World

_previous note:_ [[Popova - Notes on Complexity]]

> He identifies three awarenesses we must eventually attain in order to wake up from the core delusions that keep our lives clenched, that stand between us and kindness:
>
> > You’re not permanent.
> > 
> > You’re not the most important thing.
> > 
> > You’re not separate.
