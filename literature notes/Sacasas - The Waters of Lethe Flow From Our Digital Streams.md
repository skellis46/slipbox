# Sacasas - The Waters of Lethe Flow From Our Digital Streams

_previous note:_ [[Rosen - On the Death of Daydreaming]]

> We need certain truths ready to hand, mingling with our imagination, our thoughts, and our desires. But my interest here is not in this form of forgetting, but in the way that we enter an existential state of forgetfulness when we are immersed in our digital Lethe, in how long we allow ourselves to abide in this state, and in the effect over the course of a lifetime of such indiscriminate immersion.
