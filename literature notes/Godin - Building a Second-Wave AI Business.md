# Godin - Building a Second-Wave AI Business

_previous note:_ [[Heffernan - Wilful Blindness in AI Mode]]

> **The cheap inference model.** Not every AI application needs a frontier model. The problems worth looking for here aren’t the ones that require reasoning or nuance—instead, look for structure, pattern recognition, aggregation, and organization at scale. Form filling. Document organization. Transcription plus summarization. Matching similar records across large datasets. These problems are unglamorous but enormous in volume and largely underserved.

_reference:_ Seth Godin. "Building a Second-Wave AI Business." https://seths.blog/2026/10/but-is-it-a-business/
