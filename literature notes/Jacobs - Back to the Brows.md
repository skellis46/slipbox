# Jacobs - Back to the Brows

_previous note:_ [[L. M. Sacasas - Reading as Counter-Practice]]

> The reader who craves surprise, excess, extremity, who is impatient with work that confirms typical expectations, is often called a highbrow reader; the writer whose work consistently violates norms and transgresses standards is often called a highbrow writer.

> For a long time now there has been no genuine lowbrow reading. Those who insist on *all* their expectations being fulfilled can get that hit much more efficiently through movies, TV, Instagram, TikTok, etc.

> The tripartite brow-discourse is much less useful than a more nuanced and more detailed account of readerly expectations, one which is sensitive to the ways different genres can generate different sets of expectations, and respond to those expectations in diverse ways.

_reference:_ Alan Jacobs. "Back to the Brows." https://readwise.io/bookreview/40360500
