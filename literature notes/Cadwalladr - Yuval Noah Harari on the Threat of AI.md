# Cadwalladr - Yuval Noah Harari on the Threat of AI

_previous note:_ 

> **Yuval Harari:** Humans conquered the world with language. It's not a coincidence that the Bible says “In the beginning was the Word”. Now, for the first time, there is something that can understand and generate language better than us. Everything made of words will be taken over by AI. Then the question for religion, and for every person, is: am I made of words, or of something beyond words? If your conception of religion is that ultimately there is a text, and the text controls everything, then AI can take it over. If you want to keep some kind of freedom, to remain human, the key question for religious people, and for everyone, is: what am I beyond the words?

> And I completely agree that the breakdown of reality is one of the signs. We have the most sophisticated information technology in human history. You can talk with somebody in the Philippines – not just talk, but on the telephone, see their face. But we can no longer agree. We can no longer have a conversation. People on the other side, wherever they are, seem increasingly alien. And this is very strange.

> Think about the US in the 1960s. The differences were much harder than they are today – the era of civil rights, the Vietnam war, the cold war, the sexual revolution, the feminist revolution. And people would still talk. Something very fundamental is being lost – the human ability to actually talk with each other. That is the source of our power. We rule the world not because we are physically stronger than elephants – we are not. We rule the world because humans can cooperate in very large numbers. And now we are losing the ability to cooperate.

> **Yuval** Be part of human communities that are based on real trust between humans. If you’re not part of one, join one. Trust doesn't disappear – it migrates to the algorithms. People tell you “I don't believe journalists, the newspapers, the old media, they're lying”. But the same person has tremendous trust in the algorithms that control social media. They believe the conspiracy theories about the human journalists, and they have total trust in the algorithms. They are totally naive about the machines.
