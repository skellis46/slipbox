# Cadwalladr - Yuval Noah Harari on the Threat of AI

_previous note:_ 

> **Yuval Harari:** Humans conquered the world with language. It's not a coincidence that the Bible says “In the beginning was the Word”. Now, for the first time, there is something that can understand and generate language better than us. Everything made of words will be taken over by AI. Then the question for religion, and for every person, is: am I made of words, or of something beyond words? If your conception of religion is that ultimately there is a text, and the text controls everything, then AI can take it over. If you want to keep some kind of freedom, to remain human, the key question for religious people, and for everyone, is: what am I beyond the words?

_reference:_ Carole Cadwalladr, The Nerve. "Yuval Noah Harari on the Threat of AI: 'Fascism Isn't the Worst-Case Scenario. Fascism Is Still Human'." https://www.thenerve.news/p/yuval-noah-harari-maria-ressa-carole-cadwalladr-conversation-ai-superintelligence-fascism-regulation
