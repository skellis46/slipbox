# Verschoor - Moravec's Paradox and Being Human in the Age of AI

_previous note:_ [[Narayanan - Fact Checking Moravec's Paradox]]

> ***“The hard things are easy. The easy things are hard.”*** This curious insight, known as [Moravec’s paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moravec%27s_paradox), has been echoing through the world of AI research since the 1980s. It tells us something profound: what we assume is simple for humans (like walking, recognising a face, or making a joke) is devilishly hard for machines. And what we assume is difficult (like solving equations, playing chess, or analysing datasets) is surprisingly easy for them.
