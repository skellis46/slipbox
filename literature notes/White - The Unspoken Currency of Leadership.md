# White - The Unspoken Currency of Leadership

_previous note:_ [[White - The Economics of Trust - What You're Spending Without Knowing It]]

> Here’s the simplest way to understand trust: **it works like a bank account**.
>
> Every interaction—every conversation, every decision, every action—is either a **deposit** or a **withdrawal** in that account. There’s no neutral. You’re always making a transaction, whether you realize it or not.

> When you follow through on what you said you’d do—**that’s a deposit**.  
> When you show up on time, listen fully, give credit, own your mistakes—**deposit, deposit, deposit.**  
> These build the balance.

_reference:_ Thomas White, Unintended Consequences. "The Unspoken Currency of Leadership." https://unintendconsequences.substack.com/p/the-unspoken-currency-of-leadership
