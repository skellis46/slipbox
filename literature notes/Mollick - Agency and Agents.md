# Mollick - Agency and Agents

_previous note:_ [[Mollick - The Overhang]]

> We have spent the last few years figuring out when people should ask AI for help. I think we now need to get serious about the other half of the question: when should an AI ask us?

> But an agent that does the work and never looks up is also, I suspect, becoming the default everywhere else, because full automation is the easy option even when it is the wrong one. We need agents that know when to look up. The results will be safer, and I know they will be more human as well.

_reference:_ Ethan Mollick, One Useful Thing. "Agency and Agents." https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/agency-and-agents
