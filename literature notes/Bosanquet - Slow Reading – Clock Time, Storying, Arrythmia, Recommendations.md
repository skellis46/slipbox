# Bosanquet - Slow Reading – Clock Time, Storying, Arrythmia, Recommendations

_previous note:_ [[Maggie Berg, Barbara Seeber - Slow Professor]]

> Like Shaw and Blazek’s ‘Politics of rhythm and crisis in the slow death of higher education’, they use Lefebvre’s rhythmanalysis to frame their theorising.
>
> > Lefebvre argued that any time something happens repeatedly in a particular place and over time, a rhythm takes shape … [S]ocial space is constituted through rhythms of practice (Lefebvre, 1991 [1974]) mediated through the senses and the body (Alhadeff-Jones, 2019). Rhythmanalysis therefore attends not only to what occurs in social spaces but to how it unfolds in time: how activities repeat, vary, accelerate, pause, or collide.
