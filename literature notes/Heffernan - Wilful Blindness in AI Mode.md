# Heffernan - Wilful Blindness in AI Mode

_previous note:_

> KPMG estimates that just [26% of enterprises](https://www.pointfive.co/blog/why-companies-overspend-on-ai-the-2026-cost-visibility-gap) really know what they are spending on AI. At the same time, many client service businesses are under the cosh to reduce their prices as clients argue that surely AI must reduce the cost of work that no longer requires teams of people working together for weeks on site. Hence the tolling of bells for the billable hour. But lower your prices, deploy AI—and then what’s the plan when prices rise? How much of today’s re-engineering will mean you can’t go back? Hope is not a strategy.

_reference:_ Margaret Heffernan. "Wilful Blindness in AI Mode." https://heffernanm.substack.com/p/wilful-blindness-in-ai-mode
