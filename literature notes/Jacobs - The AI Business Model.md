# Jacobs - The AI Business Model

_previous note:_ [[Purser - AI Is Destroying the University and Learning Itself]]

> I sometimes ask family and friends: What would the big tech companies have to do, how evil would they have to become, to get The Public to abandon them? And I think the answer is: They can do anything they want and almost no one will turn aside. The passive acceptance of utter cruelty, in this venue and in others, has become the most characteristic feature of our cultural moment.

> I used this [Gahan Wilson](https://www.newyorker.com/culture/postscript/the-beautifully-macabre-cartoons-of-gahan-wilson) cartoon a while back to illustrate the ed-tech business model: the big ed-tech companies always sell universities technology that does severe damage to the educational experience, and when that damage becomes obvious they sell universities *more* tech that’s supposed to fix the problems the first bundle of tech caused.

_reference:_ Alan Jacobs. "The AI Business Model." https://blog.ayjay.org/the-ai-business-model/
