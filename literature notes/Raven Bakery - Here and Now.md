# Raven Bakery - Here and Now

_previous note:_ [[Kimmerer - Serviceberry Abundance and Reciprocity]]

> Why do I think local food is important? Because relationship is important. Because our connection to our neighbors, to our farmers, to the people who harvest our fruit and slaughter our livestock, who wash and pack, who process and deliver, who stock our grocery shelves and mop the floors and ring up our purchases is important. Because our connection to the land we live on, to the water and air and soil, to the tilled fields and wild places and the scrub forests in between, to the whole wide more than human world is important. It’s not about Ethical Consumerism (a fundamental contradiction if ever there was one). It’s not about Organic or Regenerative or counting food miles. It’s not about purity or moral certainty. It’s about belonging and obligation. It is, to borrow from Kimmerer, about reciprocity.
